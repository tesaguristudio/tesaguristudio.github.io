Privacy Policy (Piyodoko)

Effective date: October 7, 2026

"Piyodoko" (the "App") is a logic puzzle game in which you place chicks on a color-divided board. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

1. Accounts

The App does not ask for your email address, phone number or real name. To provide online features (rankings, friends, daily puzzle records), a random anonymous user ID is issued automatically on first launch (Supabase anonymous authentication). This ID is not linked to your name, email or other personal details.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Level progress, earned stars, the board of a level in progress
  • Hint balance, challenge points and their recovery time
  • Daily puzzle results and streaks
  • Settings such as sound/music volume and mascot color

This data is stored in the App's local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Information sent to and stored on our server (online features)

To provide rankings and friend features, the following is sent to and stored in a Supabase database operated by the developer:

  • Anonymous user ID
  • Username (2–12 characters, chosen by you), avatar color/badge, friend code
  • Cleared level numbers, clear time, mistakes, hints used and clear timestamps
  • Daily puzzle start/finish times, time, and the submitted placement (verified to prevent cheating)
  • Weekly score
  • Friend relationships (requests, acceptances, blocks), challenges and their results, cheers sent and received
  • Counters used for rate limiting and abuse prevention

Information visible to other players

  • Rankings: username, avatar, rank and score / clears / time (visible to all players)
  • Username search and friend code: username and avatar
  • Friends: highest level, today's daily time, weekly score and streak

Please do not use your real name or contact details as your username.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch to receive hints or challenge points. No ads are shown without your choice.

AdMob may collect the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA), IP address, device and OS information, and ad view/interaction data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, consent is obtained through Google's consent management tool before ads are served. You can change ad personalization in your device settings or via "広告の設定（プライバシー）" (Ad privacy settings) in the App.

See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

5. Camera

The camera is used only to scan a friend's QR code. Scanning happens on your device; images are neither stored nor sent.

6. Purposes of use

  • Saving game progress and providing rankings, friends, challenges and the daily puzzle
  • Preventing cheating and handling abuse
  • Serving ads
  • Investigating bugs and improving the App

7. Sharing with third parties

Except for advertising as described in Section 4 and where required by law, we do not share or sell your information to third parties.

8. Storage location and retention

Our server (Supabase) is hosted in the Tokyo region. Online feature data is kept until you request deletion. Abuse-prevention records are deleted after a limited period.

9. Deleting your information

  • Information stored on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the App.
  • You can delete your server data (username, records, friends, challenges, cheers, etc.) and the data on your device at any time from My Page → "アカウントとデータを削除" (Delete account and data) in the App.
  • If you can no longer use the App (for example, after uninstalling it), contact us at the address below with your friend code or username, and we will delete your data after verification.

10. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated due to changes in law or new features. Important changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
